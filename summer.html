Halloween + Haunted Stuff for Sasha

deep rebuild · Sep 28 · 2026 dates now live

Sasha likes genuinely spooky experiences, so this is sorted by how close each thing gets to a real haunt—not just pumpkin crafts. Age guidance is explicit where organizers publish it.

🏆 Sasha shortlist: Ascarium Sharks After Dark overnight Oct 3–4; Spooktober weekends at Prospect Park Zoo; Maze by Moonlight Oct 10 & 17; MoMI After Dark Oct 24 or Halloween; Halloween on the Farm Oct 31 with ghost stories inside the old farmhouse; and the very convenient Brooklyn Halloween cluster on Oct 31. Wild card: Haunted Hotel at Lakeside is a real walk-through haunt, but organizers recommend 13+.
