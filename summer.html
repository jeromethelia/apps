Halloween + Haunted Stuff for Sasha

deep dive · refreshed Sep 4 · new aquarium + MoMI + moonlight maze

Sasha likes genuinely spooky experiences, so this goes beyond pumpkin crafts. I’m prioritizing immersive haunted-house / ghost-story experiences that are plausible for a brave six-year-old, while clearly separating attractions whose published age guidance is older.

🏆 Best bets: Queens Farm Halloween on the Farm (ghost stories inside the historic farmhouse + maze), Prospect Park Zoo Spooktober, Luna Park Halloween Harvest, MoMI’s Halloween-week horror atmosphere/exhibitions, and—if 2026 dates appear—the Gravesend Inn. Gravesend Inn caveat: City Tech’s older official guidance called it appropriate for ages 10+, and I have not found a 2026 fall run announced yet, so it stays a watch item rather than a Sasha recommendation.
